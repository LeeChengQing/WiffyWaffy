# Wiffy Waffy menu product photos

The 10 product images correspond to the 10 product IDs in `data.js`.
The `classic-mix-alternate.webp` image is the second photographed example of the Classic flavour combination.

- `assets/products/<product-id>.webp`: optimized **640 × 640** images for cards/details on mobile.
- `assets/products/4k/<product-id>.webp`: **4096 × 4096** high-resolution export masters.
- All 22 assets were cropped from the user-provided original Wiffy Waffy menu poster (709 × 1568 screenshot) and scaled with Lanczos + mild sharpening.
- No ingredients or photo details were generated or fabricated.
- **Important**: a 4K export from a small screenshot does not recover native 4K photographic detail. Replace these images with original professional food photos when available.

The site deliberately references the smaller files to avoid loading 4K masters in every card.
